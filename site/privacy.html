Privacy notice

This notice covers the developerz.ai Designer waitlist. The waitlist form has been retired: the extension is on the Chrome Web Store and the site no longer collects email addresses. Addresses submitted before the form was retired are handled as described below.

What we collect

Nothing new. The site has no waitlist form, so it collects no email address from you. The only addresses we hold are those submitted to the waitlist before it was retired, and nothing else: no name, no tracking profile, no advertising identifiers.

Why we collect it

We no longer collect addresses. Addresses submitted before the form was retired are used solely to email you as we make updates to the developerz.ai Designer extension on the Chrome Web Store. We do not run marketing drip campaigns off this list.

Double opt-in

Addresses submitted before the form was retired joined the mailing list only after the confirmation link was clicked. An address whose link was never clicked is stored as unconfirmed: beyond the confirmation email it is not on the mailing list and gets no updates. The site no longer offers the form.

Where it is stored

On developerz.ai-operated infrastructure. Your email is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Unsubscribe

Every email we send carries a List-Unsubscribe header pointing at an email address, so your mail client may offer an unsubscribe action; some clients only open a draft, which you then need to send. You can also ask us to remove your address via developerz.ai.

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us via developerz.ai.
